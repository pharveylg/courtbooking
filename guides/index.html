Admin Access

Click Staff, enter the admin PIN. Default PIN is 1234 unless changed in Admin > Security.

Public vs Admin Tabs

  • Public users see Book, Queue, and Payment.
  • Admins unlock Bookings and Admin tabs after entering the staff PIN.
  • Use one dedicated admin device/browser because current data uses localStorage.

Confirm Reservations

  • Open Admin > Confirm & Tag Bookings.
  • Verify payment externally through GCash, Maya, BPI, or bank transfer.
  • Change Pending to Reserved only after payment is verified.
  • The app uses only two booking statuses: Pending and Reserved.

Morning Booking

  • 7AM-12PM is walk-in queue by default.
  • Toggle individual morning hours if booking is allowed.
  • Enabled hours become public bookable slots.

Open Play Blocks

  • Create date + time range entries.
  • Blocks reservations for that slot.
  • Displays as Reserved - Open Play.

Payment QR Management

  • Open Admin > Payment QR Code & Channels Manager.
  • Select GCash, Maya, or BPI / Bank.
  • Upload QR image or paste direct image URL.
  • Update account label and save.
  • Users can switch QR codes in Payment tab.

Queue Module Notes

  • Queue is public and standalone, not tied to reservations.
  • Multiple queue sessions can exist at once.
  • Supports singles, doubles, exact scores, Winner Stays, and Fixed Order.
  • Queue creator PIN protects reset, delete, and player removal.
  • Queues expire and auto-clear after midnight.
Important

This version stores data in the browser using localStorage. For shared live bookings across all users and devices, upgrade to Firebase Firestore or Realtime Database.

How to Reserve

  • Open the Book tab.
  • Check Today's Schedule or choose another date in the calendar.
  • Select start and end time.
  • Enter name, email, and group notes.
  • Click Confirm Reservation.

Booking Status

  • Pending: reservation submitted, payment not yet verified.
  • Reserved: admin verified your external payment.

Payment

  • Open Payment tab.
  • Choose GCash, Maya, or BPI / Bank.
  • Scan QR and pay externally.
  • Keep your reference screenshot.

Using The Queue Tab

  • Create or join a public queue board.
  • Choose singles or doubles.
  • Use Winner Stays or Fixed Order rotation.
  • Enter exact final scores after matches.
  • Queue creator PIN protects reset/delete/player removal.
  • Queues auto-clear after midnight.
Reminders
  • Arrive before your reserved time.
  • Bring your payment reference if staff asks for verification.
  • Morning play is usually walk-in queue unless admin enables booking.
  • Staff-reserved hours are not available for public booking.
