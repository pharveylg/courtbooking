# Player Guide

This guide is for anyone who uses the facility's booking site or a tournament link: guests and signed-in players alike. Most things work without an account.

---

## Before you start

**Open the site from your facility.** Use the booking link your facility shared with you. If you don't have one, open the site and choose your facility from the list. The site remembers your choice on that device until you tap **Switch Facility**.

**The top menu has these tabs for players:**

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Big buttons for booking and games, plus tournament tiles and the facility's contact details |
| **Book** | Reserve a court hour |
| **Find a Game** | Join or start a pickup game, or join an Open Play session |
| **Payment** | Pay and send proof. On wider screens this tab is labeled **How to Pay** |
| **Contact** | The facility's phone, email, and social links. On phones, use the **Contact facility** tile on the Home tab instead |

Some tabs are for staff. **Queue** and **Match Stats** are visible, but tapping either one asks for the facility's **Admin PIN**. Players don't need that PIN. The **Bookings** and **Admin** tabs are for staff only.

**Two kinds of PIN, two purposes:**

- **Booking PIN:** 4 digits you choose yourself when you reserve a court. You use it to pay for the reservation later (**Pay Now**), to link a reservation to a game you organize, and to cancel from the schedule. Write it down.
- **Admin PIN:** Staff only. You never need it as a player.

**You don't need an account to book, pay, join a game, or use the walk-in queue.** Signing in is optional. It keeps your list of matches on any device (see Flow 5).

**Walk-in queue:** Anyone at the court can add themselves to a walk-in queue without a PIN. See Flow 8.

## Flow 1: Book a court

**Goal**

Reserve one or more hours on a court at your facility. Your reservation starts as **Pending** until staff confirm your payment.

**Who this is for**

Players and guests. No account is needed.

**Where**

The **Book** tab. You can also tap **Book the court** on the Home tab.

**Steps**

1. Open the booking link your facility shared with you.
2. Tap **Book** in the top menu.
3. Under **Pick a date**, tap the day you want. Use the arrows to move to another month.
4. Look at the schedule for that day. Free hours show a **Book Now** button.
5. Tap **Book Now** on the hour you want.
6. Check the **Your slot** summary at the top of the form.
7. Type your **Full Name**.
8. Type your **Email**.
9. Optional: type a note in **Group / Notes**, such as "4 players."
10. If the facility has more than one court, pick a court in the **Court** list.
11. Check the **Start** and **End** times. Change them if you need a longer block.
12. Type a 4-digit **Booking PIN** and remember it.
13. Check the total at the bottom of the form.
14. Tap **Confirm Reservation**.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The **Payment** tab opens and shows a card titled **Reservation held · Pending payment**. The card lists your date, time, and amount, and reminds you to keep your Booking PIN.
- The schedule now shows your hour with a **PENDING** label and your name.
- Your reservation stays **Pending** until staff check your payment. That is normal right after you book.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"Name and email required."** Fill in both fields.
- **"Please enter a 4-digit Booking PIN."** Type exactly four digits.
- **An hour won't book.** Pick another hour. Hours that are already taken, reserved for staff, or set aside for walk-in play can't be booked online.
- **You are unsure which hours are walk-in.** The note under the schedule says which time windows are walk-in queue and which are hourly booking. Hours vary by facility.
- **You lost your Booking PIN.** Without it you can't use **Pay Now**, link a game, or cancel from the schedule. Ask the facility how they want to handle it.

**Related flows**

- Flow 2 (pay for the reservation and send proof).
- Flow 3 (link a reservation to a game is done by the game's organizer, not by other players).

## Flow 2: Pay for your reservation and send payment proof

**Goal**

Pay the facility using the QR code for your payment channel, then send proof of payment so staff can confirm your booking.

**Who this is for**

Players who have a **Pending** reservation or an Open Play spot. The **Send payment proof** button appears on the Payment tab right after you book, or after you use **Pay Now** with your Booking PIN.

**Where**

The **Payment** tab (called **How to Pay** in the top menu on wider screens).

**Steps**

1. If you are still on the Payment tab right after booking, go to step 2. If you left, tap **Book**, find your hour, tap **Pay Now**, and type your Booking PIN. The app then opens the Payment tab.
2. On the Payment tab, tap **GCash**, **Maya**, or **BPI / Bank** to show that channel's QR code.
3. Scan the QR code with your banking or e-wallet app, and send the amount shown.
4. Make sure the account name in your app matches the account name shown on the page.
5. Keep your reference number or a screenshot of the payment.
6. Tap **Send payment proof**.
7. Choose a photo or a PDF from your phone.
8. Wait for the message **"Proof uploaded — admins can now review it."**

**What you should see when it worked**

- The card changes to **Proof sent** and says: "Thanks! The club will check your payment and confirm your booking."
- Your Booking PIN stays on the card in case you need it.
- Your reservation stays **Pending** until staff mark it **Reserved** (or **Paid**). This can take some time after you send proof.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"No booking yet — reserve a slot first, then upload proof."** Book an hour first (Flow 1), then send proof.
- **"File over 3MB limit."** This message applies to PDFs and other non-image files. Photos are accepted. Use a smaller PDF, or send a photo of the page.
- **The app says the proof was saved on this phone because the connection is down.** The card will still show **Send payment proof**. Tap it again when you have a connection, and choose the file again.
- **"Incorrect Booking PIN! (N attempts left before a short cooldown)."** Check the PIN you typed. After several wrong tries the app makes you wait a short time before trying again.
- **Pay Now doesn't appear.** It shows only for hours that are still **Pending**. Hours that are already reserved don't show it.

**Related flows**

- Flow 1 (book the hour first).
- Flow 4 (Open Play spots use this same payment and proof step).

## Flow 3: Find a Game: join or start a pickup game

**Goal**

Join a game that a player has created, or start your own game and invite others. Joining a pickup game is free, and the facility does not collect money for it.

**Who this is for**

Anyone. You don't need an account. The app remembers your name and email on this device after your first join.

**Where**

The **Find a Game** tab. Use the **+ Create Game** button at the top of the list to start a game.

### Join a game

**Steps**

1. Tap **Find a Game** in the top menu.
2. Scroll the list and tap a game card to open its details.
3. Read the date, time, skill level, format, and the number of players still needed.
4. Tap **Join Game**.
5. If the app asks, type your name, then your email.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The **You're in!** screen says "Your spot is saved. See you on court."
- The game shows up under **My Games** on this device.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"This game is no longer open."** The game is full, expired, or canceled. Choose another game.
- **"Sorry, that last spot was just taken."** Someone joined just before you. Choose another game.
- **"You already joined this game."** Your spot is already saved.
- **Some games ask for payment before joining.** The first tap shows the organizer's payment terms and does not join you yet. Read the terms, then tap **Join & I'll Pay**. The payment is between you and the organizer. The facility does not process it.
- **The card has no Join button or says "Expired."** The game's start time has passed or it has been canceled.

### Create a game

**Steps**

1. Tap **+ Create Game**.
2. On the first screen, pick the **Date**.
3. Pick the **Start Time** and **End Time**.
4. Optional: pick a **Court** if the list shows one.
5. Optional: tick **Also reserve the court for this time**, then type a 4-digit **Reservation PIN**. Choose this only if you want to hold the court. The app then sends you to the Payment tab to pay for it (see Flow 2).
6. Tap **Continue**.
7. Pick the **Skill Level** and **Format** (Doubles, Singles, or Open).
8. Type **Players Needed**, from 2 to 20.
9. Optional: type a short note in **Notes**.
10. Tap **Continue**.
11. Type your **Your Name** and **Your Email**.
12. Optional: tap **+ Add another player** to add players now. Each one needs a name and an email.
13. Optional: tick **Require payment before joining**, then type an amount and a note on how to pay. This is an agreement between players. The app does not verify it.
14. Tap **Create game**.

If the app finds a similar open game at the same time, it suggests joining that one instead. Tap **Join Existing Game**, or tap **Create new game anyway**.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The **Your game is up** screen appears, and the game is listed on Find a Game.
- If you reserved a court, the app opens the Payment tab instead, with your reservation card.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"Pick a date, start time and end time."** Fill in all three.
- **"The end time must be after the start time."** Pick a later end time.
- **"That date/time has already passed."** Pick a future date or time.
- **"Players needed must be a whole number from 2 to 20."** Change the number.
- **The app asks you to wait before creating another game.** This is a limit to discourage spam. Try again later.
- **"Enter a 4-digit reservation PIN, or uncheck 'reserve the court'."** Type four digits, or untick the box.
- **"That court was just booked by someone else."** Pick another court, time, or date.

**Related flows**

- Flow 4 (Open Play sessions run by the facility are listed on the same tab and work differently).
- Flow 2 (if you reserve a court with your game).

## Flow 4: Open Play: join and pay

**Goal**

Take a spot in an Open Play session run by the facility, and pay the per-person rate.

**Who this is for**

Players. Open Play spots have a per-person rate, so you pay the facility through the Payment tab.

**Where**

The **Find a Game** tab. Open Play sessions show as cards with an **Open Play** label and a **Join & Pay** button.

**Steps**

1. Tap **Find a Game** in the top menu.
2. Tap the Open Play card you want.
3. Read the date, the game mode, and the rate for one person.
4. Tap **Join & Pay**.
5. If the app asks, type your name, then your email.
6. The app opens the **Payment** tab. Pay the amount shown, using the QR code for your payment channel.
7. Send proof of payment (Flow 2).

**What you should see when it worked**

- A message says: "Joined! Head to Payment to upload proof of [amount] — admin will confirm your spot."
- The Payment tab shows **Your spot is held · Pending payment** with the amount for one person.
- Staff confirm your spot after they check your payment. Until then it stays pending.

**If something goes wrong**

- **The card says "Walk-ins only — join in person."** The facility is not taking online joins for that session. Join at the facility instead.
- **"Public joining is restricted for this block — join in person."** The same as above, shown when you tap the button.
- **"This Open Play block is no longer open."** The session is full or has ended. Choose another session.
- **"You've already joined this block."** Your spot is already saved. Don't pay twice.
- **Your spot still shows as pending after you paid.** Wait for staff to check the payment. If it stays pending for a long time, contact the facility from the **Contact** tab.

**Related flows**

- Flow 2 (sending proof of payment).
- Flow 3 (pickup games, which are free to join).

## Flow 5: Sign in and link your account

**Goal**

Keep your list of matches and bookings on any device. Your list on this device is merged with your account, so nothing is lost.

**Who this is for**

Players who want the same list on more than one device, or who want to keep it after clearing browser data. Signing in is optional.

**Where**

The **My Account** page. It opens on the **Matches** tab. The **Profile** tab also has a **Sign in** button that takes you to the same sign-in options.

**Steps**

1. Open the **My Account** page. Returning visitors reach it automatically from the site's front page. You can also reach it from the facility picker.
2. Tap **Sign in with Google**.
3. Pick your Google account when the Google window opens.
4. Optional, for email instead of Google: tap **Use email instead**, type your email and a password of at least 6 characters, then tap **Sign in**.
5. Optional, to create an email account: tap **Create an account**, optionally type your name, type your email and password, then tap **Create account**.
6. Optional, if you forgot your password: type your email, then tap **Forgot password?**. The app sends a reset link if the email has an account.
7. Wait for the list to update.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The bar at the top changes to **Signed in as** your name or email, with **Sign out** and **Delete my saved data** buttons.
- The note under the page title says the list is saved to your account and follows you to other devices.
- Your matches from this device now appear together with anything already saved on your account.

**If something goes wrong**

- **Google sign-in fails or the window closes.** The page shows an error and offers **Try full-page Google sign-in**. You can also tap **Use email instead**.
- **"Enter your email above first, then tap Forgot password."** Type your email in the box, then tap the link again.
- **A password that is too short.** Use at least 6 characters.
- **Your notification history is missing on another device.** Notification history is kept only on the device that received the alerts. The note on the page says so.
- **You want to remove your saved list from your account.** Tap **Delete my saved data**. The page confirms first. This device keeps its own list.

**Related flows**

- Flow 6 (tournament matches and alerts).

## Flow 6: Look up your tournament matches and turn on alerts

**Goal**

Find your court and time for a tournament, and get alerts when a match is scheduled or moves.

**Who this is for**

Players registered for a tournament. You need the mobile number you gave when you registered. You don't need an account or a PIN.

**Where**

The tournament page. Open it in either of these ways:

- Tap the tournament tile on the facility's **Home** tab. The tile shows **Schedule, brackets and your matches**, or **Live now · your matches and alerts** while the tournament is running. The tile opens the page with **My matches & alerts** already expanded.
- Use the link the organizer shared with you.

The section you need is **🏓 My matches & alerts**, near the top of the page.

**Steps**

1. Open the tournament page.
2. If **My matches & alerts** is closed, tap it to open.
3. In the **Mobile number you registered with** box, type your number.
4. Tap **Show my matches**.
5. Read the **Coming up** list, and then **Results** if there are finished matches.
6. Optional: tap **Turn on alerts** in the box above your matches.
7. In the browser prompt, tap **Allow** (or the equivalent on your browser).
8. Wait for the message **Alerts are on for this device**.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The line **Showing matches for** your name or team names, followed by each match: the opponent, the division and round, and the court and time.
- Until the organizer publishes the schedule, each match says that the times will appear later. Once a court and time are set, each match shows them.
- The next match shows a countdown.
- If a match moves or gets a new time, a yellow banner says **A change to your schedule**. Tap **Got it** to clear it.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"Enter the mobile number you registered with."** Type the number with at least 10 digits.
- **"We couldn't find that number in this tournament."** Use the exact number you gave when you registered. If it still doesn't work, ask the organizer.
- **"Could not load your matches right now."** Check your connection and tap **Show my matches** again.
- **"Match alerts are blocked."** Allow notifications for this site in your browser settings, then come back and tap **Turn on alerts**.
- **iPhone or iPad says to add the page to your Home Screen.** Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open the page from the Home Screen icon, then turn on alerts.
- **"This browser can't receive alerts."** Keep the page open or bookmarked. It still shows your court and time as they change.
- **Someone else's matches are showing.** Tap **not you?** to clear the lookup on this device.

**Related flows**

- Flow 5 (sign in to keep your list on any device).
- Flow 7 (share photos from the tournament).

## Flow 7: Upload photos to the tournament gallery

**Goal**

Share photos from a tournament. An organizer reviews each photo before it appears in the public gallery.

**Who this is for**

Anyone with the upload link from the tournament organizer. No account or PIN is needed. Organizers can revoke a link, let it expire, or cap how many uploads it allows.

**Where**

The upload link. Open it on your phone or computer. It is sent to you by the organizer.

**Steps**

1. Open the upload link the organizer sent you.
2. Tap **Tap to choose or take photos**.
3. Choose one or more photos from your phone. On a phone, you can also take new ones.
4. Check the previews that appear.
5. Tap **Upload**.
6. Wait while the status line shows **Uploading N of M...**.
7. Read the result on the **Thanks!** screen.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The **Thanks!** screen shows a checkmark and the line **N photos submitted for review.**
- The photos appear in the public gallery after an organizer approves them.

**If something goes wrong**

- **The page says "Link No Longer Active."** The link was revoked, expired, or reached its upload limit. Ask the organizer for a new link.
- **"Could not verify the upload link -- check your connection and try again."** Check your connection, then tap **Upload** again.
- **A partial result, such as "2 of 5 uploaded -- 3 failed."** Tap **Retry 3 failed photos**. Only the failed photos are sent again.
- **"Upload limit reached for this link."** The link has no uploads left. Ask the organizer for a new one.
- **"This link has been revoked since you started uploading."** The organizer closed the link during your upload. Ask for a new link for the remaining photos.
- **Large camera photos.** You don't need to shrink them. The page reduces each photo before it uploads.

**Related flows**

- Flow 6 (the tournament page).

## Flow 8: Join the walk-in queue

**Goal**

Add your name to the line for an open rotation at the court. Staff or players at the court run the rotation. You join the line without signing in and without a PIN.

**Who this is for**

Anyone at the court, including guests. No sign-in, no account, and no PIN.

**Where**

The **Walk-in Queue** card, under **Quick tools** on the facility picker page. Reach the picker by opening the site's front page when no facility is remembered on this device, or by tapping **Switch Facility** on the booking site.

**Steps**

1. Open the facility picker.
2. Tap **Walk-in Queue** under **Quick tools**.
3. Check the screen. If you see a setup form with a **Start queue** button, no queue is running on this device. Ask the person running the court to start one, then come back to this step.
4. Under **Add to the line**, type your name in **Player name**.
5. If you are playing doubles, type your partner's name in **Partner (optional)**. Leave it blank for singles.
6. Tap **Add**.
7. Check the **Waiting line** list for your name.

**What you should see when it worked**

- A message says "[Your name] added to the line!"
- Your name appears in the **Waiting line** list.
- The queue board shows who is on court and the rotation as matches finish.

**If something goes wrong**

- **A pop-up asks whether your name matches someone already in the queue.** Tap **OK** to use the existing player's name, or **Cancel** to keep your name as a separate player.
- **Your name is not on the screen you see.** The queue runs on one device and one browser tab. Another phone cannot see the line. Go to the device that is running the queue.
- **The queue disappeared.** Closing the browser tab clears the queue. The facility picker says the walk-in queue is not saved and clears when you close the tab. Ask the person running the court to start a new one.
- **The rotation doesn't match what you expected.** Ask the person running the court. This guide does not cover how the rotation is changed.

**Related flows**

- Flow 1 (book a reserved court time if you want a set slot instead of the walk-in line).
