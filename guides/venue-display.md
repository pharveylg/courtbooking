# Venue Display Guide

This guide is for whoever sets up and runs the public venue screen: the TV or big screen at the courts that shows a tournament live.

---

## Before you start

**What the screen is.** A full-screen page that shows a tournament as it happens. It has four parts:

- **Header:** the facility and venue names, a **LIVE** badge when a match is being played, the session time, courts in use, and the number of finished matches.
- **On court now:** the live match, rotating when more than one match is playing.
- **Leaderboard:** the standings, for all divisions combined or for one division at a time.
- **Spotlight ticker:** short facts about the tournament, scrolling along the bottom.

**No PIN is needed to view the screen.** The screen shows the same tournament information as the public tournament page, so anyone with the link can see it.

**You need the Admin PIN once,** to open Tournament Admin and copy the link. After that, the screen runs on its own. Nobody needs to sign in to it.

**The link is available only after the tournament is published.** While a tournament is still a draft, the link is hidden and the screen says the tournament is not published yet.

---

## Flow 1: Open the venue display

**Goal**

Get the live tournament screen running on the venue TV, in full screen, and leave it running.

**Who this is for**

Venue staff setting up the screen for a tournament.

**Where**

**Tournament Admin**, in the **Announcements** section, on the link **Open Venue Display ↗**. You reach Tournament Admin from the **Go to Tournaments →** button in the Admin area of your facility's booking site. That area requires the facility Admin PIN.

**Steps**

1. Open your facility's booking site on the computer or device that is connected to the TV.
2. Open the **Admin** area and enter the facility's **Admin PIN**.
3. Tap **Go to Tournaments →** to open Tournament Admin.
4. Open the tournament you want to show.
5. Find the **Announcements** section.
6. Click **Open Venue Display ↗**. The screen opens in a new browser tab.
7. Wait for the tournament name to appear. The screen shows **Loading tournament display...** first.
8. Click **⛶ Fullscreen** in the top-right corner of the screen.
9. Move the window to the TV, or connect the device to the TV, and leave the tab open.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The header shows the facility name, the tournament name, and the venue.
- **Session time** shows **--:--:--** until the first match goes live. After that, it counts up from that moment.
- **Courts playing** shows a count such as **1/4**.
- **Completed matches** shows how many matches have finished.
- The **On court now** panel and the **Leaderboard** fill the screen, with the **Spotlight** ticker along the bottom.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"Not Published Yet"** appears. The tournament is still a draft. Publish it in Tournament Admin, then reload the screen.
- **"Tournament Not Found"** appears. The link is out of date, or the tournament was removed. Copy the link again from Tournament Admin.
- **"Could Not Load"** appears with a **Retry** button. The connection failed while loading. Tap **Retry**.
- **You don't see the Open Venue Display link.** The link appears only after the tournament is published. Check the tournament's status in Tournament Admin.
- **The Fullscreen button is missing.** This browser does not support the Fullscreen button. Use your browser's full-screen mode (on many browsers, the F11 key).
- **The TV goes to sleep.** The page asks the device to keep the screen awake. This is best effort and not guaranteed on every device. Adjust the device's power settings so the screen does not turn off.

## Flow 2: Choose Auto or Manual for the leaderboard

**Goal**

Let the leaderboard cycle through every view automatically, or hold one view on screen until you're ready to change it.

**Who this is for**

Venue staff, or anyone running the screen during the event.

**Where**

The **Leaderboard** panel, on the right side of the screen. Its view tabs sit at the top right of the panel: **Combined**, then one tab for each division, and an **Auto** button at the end.

**Steps**

1. Leave the leaderboard in **Auto** (the default). Watch it move to the next view about every 8 seconds, starting with **Combined** and then each division in order.
2. To hold one view, tap its tab (for example, a division name). The screen stops cycling and stays on that view.
3. To go back to cycling, tap **Resume Auto**. The cycle picks up from the view it was on, instead of starting over.

**What you should see when it worked**

- In **Auto**, the **Auto ●** button is shown, and the current view's tab is highlighted in lime.
- In **Manual** (after you tap a tab), the tab you tapped stays highlighted, and the button changes to **Resume Auto**.
- The leaderboard shows the top three players or teams as a podium, with medals. The rest of the standings appear in a list below the podium.
- Each row shows the rank, the name, games played (GP), wins and losses (W-L), point difference (+/-), and win percentage.
- On the **Combined** view, a colored dot next to each name shows that player's division.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"Leaderboard will appear once matches are scored."** No matches have finished yet. Only finished matches with a winner and a score count toward the leaderboard.
- **A division you were holding disappears** (for example, a division was removed). The screen goes back to **Auto** on its own.
- **A division doesn't appear as a tab.** Divisions appear once the tournament has them. Check the tournament's divisions in Tournament Admin.

## Flow 3: Read the On court now panel and the spotlight ticker

**Goal**

Know what each part of the screen shows, so you can explain it to players and spectators.

**Who this is for**

Anyone watching the screen, and the staff who keep it running.

**Where**

- **On court now:** the large panel on the left side.
- **Header counters:** the top of the screen.
- **Spotlight ticker:** the strip along the bottom.

**Steps**

1. Look at the **On court now** panel. When more than one match is live, the panel moves to the next live match about every 6 seconds. Small dots at the top right show which match is showing.
2. Read the line under the panel's title. It shows the division, then the venue and court. If a match has no court assigned yet, the line says **Court assignment pending**.
3. Read the two sides of the match: the names and the points. The side that is serving has a lime border and a **Serving** label.
4. Read the games line under the score to see the games already finished in that match.
5. Look at the **LIVE** badge in the header. It appears whenever at least one match is live.
6. Look at **Courts playing**. It counts live matches that have a court assigned. For example, **2/4** means two of four active courts have a live match.
7. Look at **Completed matches** for the number of finished matches.
8. Read the spotlight ticker along the bottom. It scrolls short facts built from finished matches. The facts can include:
   - A team or player who is unbeaten, after at least two games.
   - The player or team who has played the most games.
   - The biggest win, by point margin.
   - The closest game, if it is different from the biggest win.
   - The current leader and that leader's win percentage.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The panel shows the live match and its score, and it changes as points are scored.
- The ticker scrolls continuously. The speed adjusts to the amount of text.
- If no match is live, the panel says **No match is live right now.**
- If no match has finished, the ticker says **Spotlight stats will appear once matches are completed.**

**If something goes wrong**

- **A match is being played, but the panel says no match is live.** Only matches that are being scored live appear in the panel. Check that the match is being scored.
- **Courts playing shows a lower count than the courts you expect.** Only live matches that have a court assigned are counted. Check the match's court in the schedule.
- **The ticker is short or missing facts.** Facts come from finished matches only, so the ticker grows as matches finish.

## Flow 4: When the screen is offline or out of date

**Goal**

Tell the difference between a short hiccup and a real problem, and know what to do.

**Who this is for**

Venue staff who watch the screen during play.

**Where**

The warning appears in the header, under the facility and venue names, in red text.

**Steps**

1. Look at the warning text in the header, if one appears.
2. If the warning says **⚠ Reconnecting… live feed hit an error**, check the venue's internet connection or Wi-Fi.
3. Wait. The warning clears on its own as soon as new data arrives.
4. If the warning is still on while matches are being played, reload the page. Then tap **⛶ Fullscreen** again if the screen left full-screen mode.

**What you should see when it worked**

- The warning disappears, and the screen updates again as scores change.

**If something goes wrong**

- **"⚠ Reconnecting… last updated 42s ago"** appears. No new data has arrived for more than 30 seconds. This can also appear during a quiet period when no one is scoring. Check whether matches are being played and scored. If they are and the warning stays on, reload the page.
- **The screen shows old scores.** The screen keeps the last information it received, so the numbers may be behind. It does not blank out. Reload the page to get the latest data.
- **"Could Not Load"** replaces the screen. The first load failed. Tap **Retry**.
- **The screen stays on "Loading tournament display..."** The connection failed or the link is wrong. Check the connection, then reload. If it still doesn't load, copy the link again from Tournament Admin.

**Related flows**

- Flow 1 (opening the screen and copying the link).

## Event-day checklist

**Before the event**

1. Publish the tournament in Tournament Admin, so the **Open Venue Display ↗** link appears.
2. Open the link on the device that drives the TV, and confirm the facility and tournament names appear.
3. Tap **⛶ Fullscreen**, then confirm the screen fills the TV.
4. Confirm the leaderboard is on **Auto** (the default) unless you want one division held on screen.
5. Set the device's power settings so the screen does not sleep during play.

**During the event**

1. Check that the **LIVE** badge appears in the header when a match starts.
2. Check that the **Courts playing** count matches the courts in use.
3. Check the warning line in the header. It should be blank while matches are being scored.
4. If the warning stays on while matches are being played, reload the page (see Flow 4).
5. If you want one division held on screen, tap its tab. Tap **Resume Auto** when you are done.

**After the event**

1. Leave the screen running while the event is in progress, so finished matches stay on the leaderboard and ticker.
2. Close the tab when the event ends.

## Quick reference

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Loading tournament display...** | The screen is connecting | Wait a few seconds |
| **Not Published Yet** | The tournament is still a draft | Publish it in Tournament Admin |
| **Tournament Not Found** | The link is wrong or out of date | Copy the link again from Tournament Admin |
| **Could Not Load** | The first load failed | Tap **Retry** |
| **⚠ Reconnecting… live feed hit an error** | The live data connection failed | Check the connection and wait; reload if it stays on |
| **⚠ Reconnecting… last updated Ns ago** | No new data for more than 30 seconds | Check if matches are being scored; reload if it stays on |
| **No match is live right now.** | No match is being scored | Nothing to do; the panel updates when a match starts |
| **Court assignment pending** | The live match has no court yet | Assign the court in the schedule |
| **Leaderboard will appear once matches are scored.** | No matches have finished | Nothing to do; the board fills in as matches finish |
