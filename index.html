How payment verification works

  1. 1
    Reserve first, pay second
    Submit your reservation via the Book tab. Your booking is held with Pending status while you pay.
  2. 2
    Scan QR and complete transfer
    Use GCash, Maya, or BPI / bank mobile app. Choose your preferred QR code using the buttons on the left. Ensure the account name matches when sending.
  3. 3
    Admin verifies and tags as Reserved
    Our staff verifies external bank/e-wallet payments and tags your booking as Reserved. No automatic in-app payment processing.
