Create Tenant

Provisions a fresh tenant with default courts, PIN, and settings. If the slug already has data (e.g. created organically by a visitor), this registers it into the directory instead of overwriting it.

Control Plane

Entitlements by Tenant

Product entitlement lifecycle per tenant: trial → active → suspended → cancelled, plus an independent pause hold. Granting here mirrors the runtime flags the tenant-facing sites actually read (clients/{slug}/status/state) — the two-layer model: the platform decides what a tenant owns; the product consumes a derived gate.

TRIAL — running evaluation window ACTIVE — entitled PAUSED — hold, status unchanged SUSPENDED — product offline CANCELLED — revoked (history kept) NOT ADDED — never granted
Off (default) = current behavior: suspension pauses the Booking site only. Applies to future changes — use Diagnostics → Re-sync all to apply it retroactively.
Entitlement Matrix
Commercial Layer

Plan Catalog

A plan bundles a monthly base fee, an included confirmed-booking allowance, per-booking overage rate, court limits, and per-add-on monthly fees. Assigning a plan sets the tenant's billing formula and stamps its pricing version — later plan edits never rewrite a tenant's subscription history.

Assign Plan to Tenant

Sets the tenant's subscription (with pricing version), overwrites its billing formula from the plan, and leaves existing entitlements untouched — grant or revoke products per tenant under Entitlements.

Platform Payment Instructions (Manual QR)

Where tenants pay their platform invoices. Shown on every non-verified invoice; no payment processor is involved — you collect externally and verify manually. Stored in platformSettings/billing.

Billing Domain

Platform Invoices

Draft invoices materialize this month's usage with frozen line items (base subscription, booking overage, staff-reserve hours, add-on fees), then walk the manual payment states. A submitted payment is never auto-verified — verification is always a human action here.

Verification Queue
Payer-submitted payment claims from billing.html, awaiting a human decision. A submission never verifies itself — approve or reject each one; the invoice only moves when you act.

Known Risk Areas

This app has no per-player authentication anywhere -- Firestore security rules are the only real gate, and most of them only check that a tenant ID is *shaped* correctly, not who's asking. This list tracks where that trust model has a concrete, known consequence, and whether it's been addressed.
