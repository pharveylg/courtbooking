Notification Control Panel
  • Players opt in from the tournament page ("My matches & alerts") using the mobile number they registered with. Only people who opt in can be notified.
  • When you publish, everyone who opted in hears the schedule is live. After that, each team gets an "Up Next" alert before its match and an alert if its court or time changes.
  • If a court's previous match has run over, the "Up Next" alert for the next match waits until that match is scored.
  • Changes you make between 10 PM and 6 AM are held and sent at 7 AM, unless play is under way.
  • Phones that can't receive alerts (some iPhones, older browsers) still see live times on the same page. Use the buttons below to reach everyone else.
